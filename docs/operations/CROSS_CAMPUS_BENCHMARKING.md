# Confidential Cross-Campus Institutional Benchmarking

## Overview
A-FED computes standardized IPEDS / HESA indicator percentiles across participating campuses using SMPC. No individual student identities, headcount totals, or confidential financials are exposed.

## Standardized Indicators
1. **Retention Rate (%)**: (Retained Students / Total Students) * 100
2. **Graduation Rate (%)**: (Graduated Students / Total Students) * 100
3. **Student-Faculty Ratio**: Total Students / Total Faculty
4. **Expenditure per Student ($)**: Total Operational Expenditure / Total Students
5. **Energy Efficiency**: kWh per Square Meter
6. **Average Academic GPA**: Mean GPA score across active cohorts
