## 2. Notification Categories & Consent Rules

1. **Transactional (Essential Academic & Safety)**:
   - Exam schedules, grade releases, fee invoices, class cancellations.
   - Consent: Implied educational public task (GDPR Art. 6(1)(e)).
2. **Behavioral Interventions**:
   - Attendance drop warnings, fee overdue reminders, LMS inactivity nudges.
   - Consent: Managed via student/parent portal preferences.
3. **Institutional Bulletins & Community**:
   - Campus festivals, guest lectures, alumni newsletters.
   - Consent: Explicit opt-in required (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).
