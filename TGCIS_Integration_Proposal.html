Executive Summary

01. Executive Summary

Definitive Finding: After thorough technical verification of the ThaibaHive codebase, we confirm that 9 out of 10 core features proposed for TGCIS by ZiQX Creative Clique are already built, functional, and production-ready in ThaibaHive. The 10th feature (timetable) requires only a dedicated database table and UI component — estimated at 2–3 days of development.

The Thaiba Garden Management Committee received a proposal from ZiQX Creative Clique to build a standalone application specifically for TGCIS (Thaiba Garden College of Integrated Studies) at a cost of ₹3,78,000 with a 20-week (5-month) delivery timeline.

This proposal demonstrates that building a separate application is unnecessary, financially wasteful, and strategically counterproductive. All proposed TGCIS features can be integrated into the existing ThaibaHive platform within 2 to 3 weeks at zero additional software cost, immediately benefiting not just TGCIS but all 23+ institutions under the Thaiba Garden umbrella.

Integration Roadmap

07. Three-Week Integration Roadmap

Week 1: Public Portal & Student Intake

Build dynamic public pages under /portal/[institutionCode] with TGCIS overview, vision, and downloadable prospectuses. Create Public Student Admission Enquiry Form submitting to student_enquiries table. Enhance Student Management with Excel/CSV bulk onboarding with row-level validation.

Days 1–5

Week 2: Timetable & Circular Compliance

Build the Interactive Timetable Grid (/academic/timetable) with class-wise and teacher-wise period scheduling and temporary teacher substitution logger. Expand Circulars Tracking with campus assignment selection and Coordinator checklist for Pending/Completed status.

Days 6–10

Week 3: Examinations, PDF Generation & UAT

Wire examination tabulation and mark moderation into dynamic PDF generation (Hall Tickets with QR, Term Report Cards, Certificates). Verify role scopes across all workflows. Run complete test suite and deliver staging build for TGCIS pilot.

Official Recommendation

10. Official Recommendation

RECOMMENDATION: Decline External Vendor Proposal

Based on comprehensive technical verification of the ThaibaHive codebase, we formally recommend that Thaiba Garden Management DECLINE the ZiQX Creative Clique proposal of ₹3,78,000 / 20 weeks, and instead APPROVE the integration of TGCIS features into the existing ThaibaHive platform.

Recommended Actions

1. Formally Decline the ZiQX Proposal

Communicate to ZiQX Creative Clique that Thaiba Garden has decided to leverage its existing ThaibaHive platform for TGCIS management, rendering the standalone application unnecessary.

2. Approve ThaibaHive Integration Budget

Allocate internal development resources for the 3-week integration sprint. Primary cost is developer time (already employed) — zero external software licensing costs.

3. Schedule TGCIS Pilot Launch

Target date: 3 weeks from approval. TGCIS becomes the flagship academic institution on ThaibaHive, demonstrating the platform's capability to all other Thaiba Garden institutions.

4. Plan Phased Rollout to All Institutions

Following TGCIS success, systematically onboard all Thaiba Garden schools and institutions onto ThaibaHive over the next 6 months.

Expected Outcomes:
  • ₹3,78,000 saved (direct vendor cost)
  • 17–18 weeks saved (faster time to deployment)
  • All 23+ institutions benefit (not just TGCIS)
  • Unified data, authentication, and reporting
  • Zero vendor dependency for future updates
  • Scalable architecture for institutional growth
